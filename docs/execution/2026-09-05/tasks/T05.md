## Required change

- Introduce a closed active/outcome-unknown/quarantined service state; arm it before a possibly durable catalog mutation and clear only after exact readback.
- Invalidate handles/continuations and refuse queries, mutations, GC and READY claims while unresolved. Do not reinterpret failure as rollback or blindly retry.
- Bound log reads before allocation and append growth before writing. Reject unterminated/corrupt tails consistently across readers.
- Keep explicit disposable-root recovery separate from normal serving. Preserve the a5abdf7 missing-catalog and no-delete protections; T11 later removes the legacy write path.

## Discriminating tests

- Inject partial write, flush error and post-write readback failure; subsequent search/retire/GC cannot use stale memory.
- Process death at each log boundary; reopen never invents empty state.
- Corrupt/oversized/unterminated log produces bounded rejection with original bytes unchanged.
