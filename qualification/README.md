Files retained here describe their original revisions. The documentation landing does not remove main's old
qualification inputs or tooling, and does not turn historical reports into current PASS results.

Use the [registry](../docs/audit/ANALYSIS_REGISTRY.md#evidence-status) for current evidence and the
[delivery sequence](../docs/implementation/README.md#delivery-sequence) for implementation. These files cannot
supply constructor credentials, activation gates or a new acceptance platform.

Record new checks with exact commit, command, platform/backend and raw outcome in the executing issue or PR.
Compiler/Clippy, live Qdrant, Windows durability, recovery, relevance and scale are separate claims. Remove old
fixtures and metadata with their actual consumers, not as a side effect of publishing documentation.
