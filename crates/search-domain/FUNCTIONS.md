# Decisions stay with their concrete owner

The unused global domain Rust package, manifest and exclusive fixtures are removed.
Current publication, source, storage and query owners make their own effect-specific decisions.

Preserve each actual cause and uncertainty boundary. Do not wrap direct outcomes in shared Decision,
reason, fingerprint, grant, eligibility or transition algebra. Shared immutable identity values remain
in [contracts](../search-contracts/FUNCTIONS.md); owned state remains with its real consumer.
