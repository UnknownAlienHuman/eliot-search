# Measurements of the working search consumer

The unused evaluation receipt/verdict Rust package, manifest and exclusive fixtures are removed.
Measurements belong to actual working commands and the current native/consumer evidence.

Keep source/binary/dependency scope and observed outcomes explicit. Missing or unfinished evidence is
unknown, not a successful result. Source/query text and keys do not belong in ordinary diagnostics.
No evaluation service, acceptance receipt, generic metric registry or test-only replacement backend.
